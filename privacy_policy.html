Space Coast Trivia
Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Space Coast Trivia (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our free Android application. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Space Coast Trivia is a free mobile quiz app about space history (Mercury, Gemini, Apollo, and Florida’s Space Coast). It is published on Google Play under the developer account associated with 1950.rockets.spacecoast@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

The App is designed to minimize personal data collection.

2.1 Information stored on your device

  • Purchase / premium status — whether you own the optional Ad-Free Mission Pass (so ads stay off after purchase or reinstall restore).
  • Optional analytics preference — whether you opted in or out of anonymous usage analytics.
  • Gameplay state — temporary in-app progress needed to run quizzes (scores, current question, etc.).

This data is stored locally on your device using standard app storage mechanisms.

2.2 Optional usage analytics (only if you opt in)

After you finish a quiz, we may ask for permission to collect limited, anonymous usage events (for example: pack selected, quiz started, question answered, quiz completed). These events help us improve content and the app experience.

  • Analytics are off by default until you choose to allow them.
  • You can change this preference later in the App’s settings (More tab).
  • We do not use this feature to build a personal profile for advertising.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob (and related Google advertising services). Google and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google’s policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

You can remove ads by purchasing the optional Ad-Free Mission Pass (one-time in-app purchase).

2.4 In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (including the Ad-Free Mission Pass) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Google handles payment processing under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

We only receive purchase status information needed to unlock ad-free access and to restore purchases on the same Google account.

2.5 Accounts

The App does not require you to create a Space Coast Trivia account or sign in with us. Google account sign-in may be used by Google Play for purchases and distribution, which is controlled by Google.

3. How we use information

  • Operate the quiz experience on your device
  • Show ads (unless you purchased ad-free)
  • Process and restore in-app purchases
  • Improve the App if you opt in to analytics
  • Respond to support requests if you email us

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that power the App:

  • Google Play / Google LLC — app distribution, payments, ads (AdMob), and related services

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not share analytics data with third parties for their own marketing if you have not opted in; when ads are shown, Google’s advertising partners may process data as described in Google’s policies.

5. Data retention

  • Local app preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.
  • Purchase records are retained by Google as needed to provide Play services and restore entitlements.
  • If remote analytics are enabled in a future update, aggregated/anonymous events may be retained only as long as needed to improve the App.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience trivia product and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Your choices

  • Ads: Purchase Ad-Free Mission Pass to remove ads, or adjust device ad/privacy settings where available (for example, advertising ID / privacy options on Android).
  • Analytics: Decline the optional prompt, or turn analytics off in the App settings.
  • Purchases: Manage through Google Play (subscriptions are not required for the current Ad-Free pass; it is a one-time product).
  • Uninstall: Removes local App data from your device (Play purchase history remains with Google).

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to a consumer mobile app (including reliance on Google Play and platform security). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. International users

The App may be used in different countries. Data processed by Google services may be handled in the United States or other locations where Google operates.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
