Request deletion of your data

Space Coast Trivia (Android app on Google Play)
Developer contact: 1950.rockets.spacecoast@gmail.com
Last updated: July 20, 2026

This page explains how users of Space Coast Trivia can request deletion of data related to the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at 1950.rockets.spacecoast@gmail.com with the subject line: Space Coast Trivia – Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your Google Play email (or the email you used to contact us) and a short note that you want your Space Coast Trivia data deleted.
  3. Uninstall the app from your device. This removes local app data stored on that device (preferences, local analytics opt-in status, and local gameplay state).
  4. We will confirm your request by email, usually within 30 days (often sooner).

What data we can delete or help clear

  • Data on your device: Uninstalling Space Coast Trivia deletes local app storage on that device (for example: ad-free status flags stored in the app, optional analytics preference, temporary quiz state).
  • Data we hold: Space Coast Trivia does not require you to create an app account. We do not operate a separate user profile database for sign-in. If you emailed us for support, we can delete that email correspondence on request.
  • Optional analytics: If you opted in to anonymous usage analytics, uninstalling removes local event storage on the device. If a future version sends events to our systems, we will delete associated records we control when you request it.

What may be retained (and by whom)

  • Google Play purchases: Purchase records for the optional Ad-Free Mission Pass are handled by Google Play. We cannot erase Google’s payment history. You can review purchases in your Google account / Play subscription & purchase settings.
  • Advertising (AdMob): Ads in the free version are provided by Google AdMob. Advertising identifiers and related ad data are processed under Google’s policies. Manage ad personalization in your device / Google account ad settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Legal retention: We may keep limited records if required by law (for example, resolving a dispute or fraud prevention), only as long as necessary.

Summary

Steps: email 1950.rockets.spacecoast@gmail.com → uninstall the app → we confirm.
Deleted: local app data on your device; support emails we hold, on request.
Not fully controlled by us: Google Play purchase records and Google ad data.

Privacy Policy: if you also published a privacy policy page, link it from your store listing and site menu.
